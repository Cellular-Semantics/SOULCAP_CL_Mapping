# B Cell Marker Literature Support

Evidence for SOULCAP B cell marker definitions, extracted verbatim from open-access papers.
Papers marked [PAYWALLED] were inaccessible: the Nature Immunology 2021 review
(PMID 34725481), the Blood 2020 comprehensive analysis (DOI 10.1182/blood.2019002782),
and the Clinical and Experimental Immunology 2021 DN B cell paper (PMID 34075582)
all returned gated or payment-required responses. Verbatim quotes below come from
five fully open-access sources.

---

## Markers with evidence

### CD19 and CD20 (required positive — pan-B cell markers)

Context: CD19 is expressed on all B-lineage cells from pro-B to memory stage; CD20 is
expressed from pre-B to memory but is lost as cells commit to the antibody-secreting
(plasmablast/plasma cell) fate. Either marker can gate the B cell compartment, but CD19
is more stable across differentiation and therefore preferred when including ASCs.

> "Mature B cells typically express both CD19 and CD20."
— Griffin & Rothstein 2012 (PMID: 22654880; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2012.00122)

> "as B cells differentiate to become plasmablasts and plasma cells, CD20 is lost but CD19
> remains, as CD38/CD138 is acquired"
— Griffin & Rothstein 2012 (PMID: 22654880; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2012.00122)

> "CD19+ B cells were then enriched to >98% using CD19 microbead magnetic separation"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

> "to positively select intact cells (Ir191+Ir193+), to positively select live cells
> (Rh103−Ir191+Ir193+), and to identify CD19+ and/or CD20+ B cells"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)
[mass cytometry (CyTOF) gating strategy; Ir/Rh channels are isotope labels, not surface markers]

### CD27 (memory vs naive distinguisher — positive on memory B cells)

Context: CD27, a TNF receptor superfamily member, is the canonical human memory B cell
marker. Naive and transitional B cells are CD27−; classical memory B cells are CD27+.
Double-negative (DN) B cells are IgD−CD27−, lacking both the naive marker IgD and the
memory marker CD27.

> "transitional (IgD+CD27−CD10+), and naïve (IgD+CD27−CD10−)"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

> "**M**: CD19+ CD27+ CD38−/lo" [memory B cell gating entry] and
> "**N**: CD19+ CD27− CD38− CD24− IgD+" [naive B cell gating entry, Table 1]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "Double negative (DN) B cells, also known as atypical memory B cells, constitute a B cell
> population that lacks expression of immunoglobulin D and CD27 memory marker."
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

### CD38 (differentiation marker — high on transitional B cells and antibody-secreting cells)

Context: CD38 expression is graded across B cell differentiation: highest on T1 transitional
cells and antibody-secreting cells (ASCs, including plasmablasts and plasma cells);
intermediate on later transitional subsets; low-to-negative on mature naive and memory B cells.
This gradient makes CD38 a key axis in B cell panel design.

> "T1 (CD38+++CD24hiCD10++IgDlo/−), T2 (CD38++CD24hiCD10+IgD+), and
> T3 (CD38+CD24+IgD+ABCB1−)"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

> "Co-expression of high levels of CD24 and CD38 have also frequently been used to identify
> them, and it is important that CD27 be included if this is the case"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)
[refers to transitional B cells]

> "**ASC**: CD19+ CD27+ CD38hi" and "**M**: CD19+ CD27+ CD38−/lo" and
> "**Tr**: CD19+ CD27− CD38int CD24+" [gating table entries, Table 1]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

### CD10 (transitional B cell marker — high on T1/T2; low on T3; negative on mature)

Context: CD10 (CALLA, common acute lymphoblastic leukemia antigen) marks immature and
transitional B cells in the periphery and is downregulated as cells reach the mature naive
stage. The T1 subset has the highest CD10 expression; T3 is CD10−; mature naive B cells
are CD10−.

> "These cells express IgD and CD10 alongside the IgM BCR so can be identified as
> IgD+ CD27−CD10hi/+"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

> "T1 (CD38+++CD24hiCD10++IgDlo/−), T2 (CD38++CD24hiCD10+IgD+), and
> T3 (CD38+CD24+IgD+ABCB1−)"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

> "transitional (IgD+CD27−CD10+), and naïve (IgD+CD27−CD10−)"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

> "CD21lo Tr cells expressed higher levels of markers of the early transitional compartment
> (IgM, CD24, CD10), but less CD21 and CXCR5"
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

### CD24 (differentiation marker — high on transitional; low on naive; negative on DN B cells)

Context: CD24 (heat-stable antigen, HSA) is co-expressed with CD38 at high levels on
transitional B cells. Its expression decreases as cells mature to naive stage and is absent
on double-negative B cells. CD24 alongside CD38 is used to resolve the transitional B cell
compartment, and CD24 negativity helps distinguish naive and DN B cells.

> "Co-expression of high levels of CD24 and CD38 have also frequently been used to identify
> them, and it is important that CD27 be included if this is the case"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)
[refers to transitional B cells]

> "**Tr**: CD19+ CD27− CD38int CD24+" [transitional B cell gating entry, Table 1] and
> "**N**: CD19+ CD27− CD38− CD24− IgD+" [naive B cell gating entry] and
> "**DN**: CD19+ CD27− CD38− CD24− IgD−" [double-negative B cell gating entry]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

### IgD (class-switch indicator — positive on naive; negative on class-switched memory)

Context: IgD is co-expressed with IgM on the surface of naive and some transitional B cells.
It is lost during class-switch recombination. Unswitched memory B cells (SOULCAP:
Unswitched, IgM-only) retain IgD or IgM surface expression; switched memory cells lose
both. IgD negativity is also the defining criterion of double-negative B cells.

> "naïve B cells express immunoglobulin (Ig)D and IgM and undergo isotype switching to
> produce IgM, IgA, IgG, or IgE exclusively"
— McGrath, Li & Wilson 2022 (PMID: 35393268; DOI: 10.1016/j.it.2022.03.005)

> "**N**: CD19+ CD27− CD38− CD24− IgD+" [naive B cell gating entry, Table 1]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "**usM**: IgM+ IgD+" [unswitched memory] / "**mM**: IgM+ IgD−" [IgM-only memory] /
> "**sM**: IgM− IgD−" [switched memory] [gating table entries, Table 1]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "Double negative (DN) B cells, also known as atypical memory B cells, constitute a B cell
> population that lacks expression of immunoglobulin D and CD27 memory marker."
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "T2 (CD38++CD24hiCD10+IgD+), and T3 (CD38+CD24+IgD+ABCB1−)"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)
[IgD present on T2 and T3 transitional cells; absent or low on T1]

### IgM, IgG, IgA, IgE (class-switch subtype markers on switched memory B cells)

Context: After class-switch recombination, memory B cells express exactly one antibody heavy
chain isotype. Unswitched memory cells co-express IgM with IgD; IgM-only memory cells
express IgM without IgD; class-switched memory cells have lost both IgM and IgD in favour
of IgG, IgA, or IgE. The SOULCAP IgA, IgG, and IgE subtypes subdivide the switched
memory (IgD−IgM−) gate by the newly acquired isotype.

> "naïve B cells express immunoglobulin (Ig)D and IgM and undergo isotype switching to
> produce IgM, IgA, IgG, or IgE exclusively"
— McGrath, Li & Wilson 2022 (PMID: 35393268; DOI: 10.1016/j.it.2022.03.005)

> "CD73+ MBCs are also proportionally more isotype-switched than CD73– MBCs, allowing them
> to produce IgG and IgA antibodies to facilitate a more diverse set of effector functions"
— McGrath, Li & Wilson 2022 (PMID: 35393268; DOI: 10.1016/j.it.2022.03.005)

> "cluster 4 was IgG1 (IGHG1) dominant with minimal contributions from other isotypes,
> cluster 7 was more balanced but with a slight bias towards IgA (IGHA1/IGHA2) overall"
— McGrath, Li & Wilson 2022 (PMID: 35393268; DOI: 10.1016/j.it.2022.03.005)
[single-cell transcriptomics of memory B cell clusters]

> "**usM**: IgM+ IgD+" [unswitched memory] / "**mM**: IgM+ IgD−" [IgM-only memory] /
> "**sM**: IgM− IgD−" [switched memory, IgG/IgA/IgE positive by exclusion] [Table 1]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "Consistent with the B cell antigen receptor-sequencing data, SARS-CoV-2-specific
> responsiveness was broad, with participation of IgM, IgA and IgG antibodies."
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

### CD138 / Syndecan-1 (plasma cell marker — distinguishes PC from PB)

Context: CD138 (Syndecan-1, SDC1) is a heparan-sulphate proteoglycan upregulated as
plasmablasts (CD138−CD38hi) mature into plasma cells (CD138+CD38hi). Within the
SOULCAP panel the CD138 axis separates PC (CD138+) from PB (CD138−) within the
CD19+CD27+/hiCD38hi gate. CD20 is simultaneously lost at the plasmablast stage.

> "as B cells differentiate to become plasmablasts and plasma cells, CD20 is lost but CD19
> remains, as CD38/CD138 is acquired"
— Griffin & Rothstein 2012 (PMID: 22654880; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2012.00122)

> "ASC responses in ICU-C patients were highly enriched in more mature CD138+ cells relative
> to both OUT-C and HDs."
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "**CD138neg ASC**: CD138−" and "**CD138pos ASC**: CD138+" [gating labels distinguishing
> plasmablasts from plasma cells within the CD19+CD27+CD38hi ASC gate]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

### CD21 / Complement Receptor 2 (DN B cell subtype marker — positive on BDN1/BDN4, negative on BDN2/BDN3)

Context: CD21 (complement receptor 2, CR2) is expressed on follicular and marginal-zone
B cells. Among double-negative (IgD−CD27−) B cells, CD21 expression subdivides BDN1
and BDN4 (CD21+) from BDN2 and BDN3 (CD21−). CD21 negativity co-occurs with CD11c
positivity in the extrafollicular BDN2 subset.

> "DN1: CD11c− CD21+" / "DN2: CD11c+ CD21−" / "DN3: CD11c− CD21−" [gating table]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "CD19+IgD−CD27−CD21+ CD11c− T-bet− CXCR5+" [DN1 phenotype, Table 1] and
> "CD19+IgD−CD27−CD21− CD11c+ T-bet+CXCR5−" [DN2 phenotype] and
> "CD19+IgD−CD27−CD21− CD11c−" [DN3 phenotype]
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "The gradual loss of CD10, CD5, and IgM and the upregulation of CD22, CD44, CD21, and
> CD23 as cells progress from immature to transitional (TI to T2 to T3) to mature naïve cells"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)
[CD21 upregulation during B cell maturation provides additional context for CD21 as a
maturity marker, contrasting with its loss in BDN2/BDN3]

### CD11c / ITGAX (DN B cell type 2 marker; also expressed on T-bet+ age-associated B cells)

Context: CD11c (integrin alpha-X, ITGAX) is a hallmark of the extrafollicular BDN2 subset
of double-negative B cells and of age-associated B cells (ABCs) / atypical memory B cells
observed in malaria, SLE, and COVID-19. Among DN B cells, CD11c positivity defines BDN2
exclusively; BDN1, BDN3, and BDN4 are all CD11c−.

> "DN2 B cells are a unique double-negative population (IgD−CD27−) that displays
> extrafollicular characteristics, such as the lack of CXCR5 and CD62L expression."
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "CD19+IgD−CD27−CD21− CD11c+ T-bet+CXCR5−" [DN2 phenotype, Table 1]
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "DN1: CD11c− CD21+" / "DN2: CD11c+ CD21−" / "DN3: CD11c− CD21−" [gating table]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "six distinct circulating MBC phenotypes were found that were definable based on the
> combinatorial expression of CD27, CD73, CD95, CD19, CD11c, and the CD45 isoform CD45RB"
— McGrath, Li & Wilson 2022 (PMID: 35393268; DOI: 10.1016/j.it.2022.03.005)

### CD185 / CXCR5 (DN B cell type 1/4 marker; also marks follicular B cells)

Context: CXCR5 (CXC chemokine receptor 5, CD185) directs lymphocytes into germinal
centres in secondary lymphoid organs (follicular homing). Among double-negative B cells,
CXCR5 positivity is the key feature of BDN1 (and by extension BDN4), which are follicular-
like. BDN2 and BDN3 are CXCR5−, reflecting their extrafollicular nature.

> "The CXCR5+ subgroup, now known as DN1, is the DN sub-population that expands in
> elderly healthy individuals, while the CXCR5- subgroup constitutes the extrafollicular
> DN2 subset."
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "DN2 B cells are a unique double-negative population (IgD−CD27−) that displays
> extrafollicular characteristics, such as the lack of CXCR5 and CD62L expression."
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "CD19+IgD−CD27−CD21+ CD11c− T-bet− CXCR5+" [DN1 phenotype, Table 1]
— Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021 (PMID: 34964023; DOI: 10.31138/mjr.32.3.192)

> "CD21lo Tr cells expressed higher levels of markers of the early transitional compartment
> (IgM, CD24, CD10), but less CD21 and CXCR5"
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "a decrease in follicular homing predisposition through CXCR5 and a corresponding increase
> in CXCR3" [describing extrafollicular B cell responses in COVID-19 severity]
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

### CD14, CD3, CD56 (exclusion markers for B cell gate)

Context: CD14 (monocyte), CD3 (T cell), and CD56 (NK cell) markers are used in a "dump
channel" to exclude non-B lineage cells from the B cell gate. The SOULCAP B cell gate
additionally excludes CD33 (myeloid) and CD64 (high-affinity Fc receptor on macrophages).
The quotes below confirm CD14, CD3, and CD56 usage as negative-selection markers;
direct verbatim evidence for CD33 and CD64 was not found in the accessible papers (see
below).

> "B cells were enriched using negative selection of CD2, CD3, CD14, CD16, CD36, CD42b,
> CD56, CD66b and CD123."
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "Cells were gated as CD3−CD14−CD16−CD19+CD38+CD27+ single live cells"
— Woodruff et al. 2020 (PMID: 33028979; DOI: 10.1038/s41590-020-00814-z)

> "a population of enriched B cells (CD3−CD14−) was collected"
— Martin et al. 2016 (PMID: 27994589; DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2016.00546)

## Markers with no direct evidence found

- **CD33 / Siglec-3**: No verbatim sentence found in the five accessible papers. CD33 is a
  myeloid-lineage antigen used in the SOULCAP B cell dump channel. Its use as a B cell
  exclusion marker is standard practice in high-parameter flow cytometry panels but was not
  stated verbatim in the fetched texts.
- **CD64 / FcγRI (FCGR1A)**: No verbatim sentence found. CD64 marks monocytes and
  macrophages and is included in the SOULCAP B cell gate as an exclusion marker. As with
  CD33, its use is established convention but was absent from the open-access papers
  retrieved here.
- **BDN4 IgE+ subtype**: No verbatim characterisation found of a fourth DN B cell subset
  distinguished by IgE surface expression. All three papers covering DN B cells (Woodruff
  et al. 2020, Sachinidis & Garyfallos 2021, and the Frontiers transitional B cell review)
  describe a three-subset model (DN1/DN2/DN3). The SOULCAP BDN4 definition
  (CD21+ CD11c− CXCR5+ IgE+) extends the DN1 phenotype by IgE surface staining;
  literature support for this specific IgE-positive split was not found in the accessible
  open-access corpus. A targeted search of Wehr et al. or Sanz et al. publications (which
  first proposed 4-subset DN B cell classifications) may supply this evidence.

# ILC Marker Literature Support

Evidence for SOULCAP innate lymphoid cell (ILC) marker definitions, extracted verbatim from open-access papers.

## Markers with evidence

### CD127 / IL-7Rα (required positive — pan-ILC marker)

IL-7 receptor alpha (CD127) is the canonical positive selection marker that defines all helper-ILC subsets and distinguishes them from cytotoxic NK cells.

> "total ILCs being defined as LIN− CD45+ CD127+"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "By first gating on the total ILC population (defined as single cells that were CD45+ Lin-CD127+), these cells could then be divided into their respective ILC subsets"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "CD45+Lin–CD3–CD161+CD127+ cells and are subdivided into 3 subsets, namely, group 1 ILCs (ILC1s) (CD117–CRTH2–), group 2 ILCs (ILC2s) (CRTH2+), and group 3 ILCs (ILC3s) (CD117+CRTH2–)."
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

> "characterized by the expression of the pan‐ILC markers CD127, CD161, and CD200R1"
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

> "The population positive for CD7, CD45, CD127 and CD161 and negative for LIN2 was separated and divided into CRTH2 positive/NKp46 negative and CRTH2 negative/NKp46 positive."
— Bento et al 2025 (PMID unknown; DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1568875)

### CD3 (required exclusion — separates ILC from T cells)

CD3 negativity is used to exclude T cells before gating on ILC subsets; it is sometimes added to the lineage cocktail and sometimes applied as a standalone gate.

> "We gated for lymphocytes, excluded doublets and dead cells and selected CD45+CD3− cells"
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

> "CD45+Lin–CD3–CD161+CD127+ cells termed ILCs"
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

> "CD3− cells were selected for lineage marker negative (Lin−) status using antibodies against CD3, CD4, TCRαβ, and TCRγδ (T cells), CD19 (B cells) CD14 (monocytes)"
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

---

### CD161 (NKR-P1; common ILC co-marker)

CD161 is widely used alongside CD127 to define bona fide ILCs and is described as a pan-ILC marker, though it is not included in the SOULCAP required-positive gate for bulk ILC or the three subsets.

> "CD45+Lin–CD127+CD161+ cells (ie, bona fide ILCs)"
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

> "Within the Lin−CD127+ population, conventional ILCs expressed CD161 but not CD94"
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

> "CD161, which is known to be present on NK cells, NKT cells, and T cells, is also expressed by human ILCs."
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

> "ILC1s were identified through the expression of CD7, CD45, CD127, CD161, absent/low NKp46 and absent LIN2, and CRTH2."
— Bento et al 2025 (PMID unknown; DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1568875)

**Caveat:** CD161 is shared with NK cells, NKT cells, and some T cells, so it must be combined with CD127 and a Lin-exclusion gate to select ILCs specifically.

### CD294 / CRTH2 (required positive on ILC2; required negative on ILC1 and ILC3)

CRTH2 (prostaglandin D2 receptor 2, encoded by *PTGDR2*) is the defining positive marker for ILC2 and is negative on ILC1 and ILC3.

> "group 2 ILCs (ILC2s) (CRTH2+), and group 3 ILCs (ILC3s) (CD117+CRTH2–)"
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

> "ILC2s as CRTH2+ CD117−/+"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "ILC2 were identified through the positive expression of CD7, CD45, CD127, CD161, CRTH2, absent/low NKp46 and absent LIN2."
— Bento et al 2025 (PMID unknown; DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1568875)

> "ILC3 were identified through the positive expression of CD7, CD45, CD127, CD161 and NKp46 and absence of CRTH2 and LIN2."
— Bento et al 2025 (PMID unknown; DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1568875)

> "ILC1s as CD117− CRTH2−"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

### CD117 / c-Kit (required positive on ILCp and ILC3; required negative on ILC1; low/negative on ILC2)

CD117 (c-Kit, *KIT*) distinguishes ILC3 (positive) from ILC1 (negative) within the CRTH2-negative compartment, and marks ILC progenitors (ILCp). ILC2 is characteristically CD117 low or negative.

> "could be subdivided into CD117−NKp44− ILC1s and CD117‐expressing NKp44− ILC3s and NKp44+ ILC3s."
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

> "ILC1s as CD117− CRTH2−" … "ILC3s as CRTH2− CD117+"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "ILC2s as CRTH2+ CD117−/+"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "ILC3 subpopulations were evaluated in whole blood using the NKp44 (44.189) antibody to defined ILC3as as CRTH2− CD117+ NKp44− and ILC3bs as CRTH2− CD117+ NKp44+"
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "group 3 ILCs (ILC3s) (CD117+CRTH2–)"
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

**Discrepancy flag — Bento et al 2025:** The Frontiers paper (bone marrow / MDS context) states: "Although CD117 (c-KIT) is commonly included in gating strategies to enhance the discrimination among ILC subsets, being highly expressed in ILC progenitors and ILC1, less so in ILC2, and absent in ILC3—we chose not to rely on this marker in our analysis. This decision was based on the context of our study, which involves comparing healthy bone marrow with bone marrow from Myelodysplastic patients, where c-KIT expression may be altered due to potential mutations." The CD117 pattern asserted here (high on ILC1, absent on ILC3) is the reverse of the consensus established by Alkon et al and Bonne-Année et al, and contradicts the SOULCAP definitions. The paper explicitly excluded CD117 from their own gating to avoid c-Kit mutation artefacts in MDS; the bracketed characterisation may contain a transcription error. The standard peripheral-blood consensus (ILC1 CD117−, ILC3 CD117+) should be followed for SOULCAP.

### CD56 / NCAM (ILCp required positive; ILC1/ILC2 required negative; ILC3 CD56+/-)

CD56 is included in lineage-exclusion cocktails in multiple protocols to remove NK cells from the ILC gate. Its optional positive expression on ILC3 subsets is noted separately.

> "(*) LIN2 is cocktail of the following antibodies: CD3-FITC (SK7), CD14-FITC (SJ25C1), CD19-FITC (L27), CD20-FITC (MφP9), and CD56-FITC (NCAM16.2)."
— Bento et al 2025 (PMID unknown; DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2025.1568875)

> Lineage markers include: "CD3, CD4, CD8, CD14, CD16, CD19, CD56, CD11c, CD11b, FcεR1a" [Table 2]
— Bonne-Année, Bush & Nutman 2019 (PMID:31586075; DOI:10.1038/s41598-019-50308-8)

> "Similar to ILC3s, CD94+ ILCs partly expressed NKp44 and IL‐1R1, and also CD117, CD56, NKG2A"
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

**Caveat:** CD56 in the lineage gate removes NK cells (CD56+ CD127−). ILC3 can express CD56 at variable levels, which is why SOULCAP encodes ILC3's CD56 as "+/−" rather than requiring it or excluding it. The ILCp gate requires CD56+, which in combination with CD117+ and CD127+ separates it from NK cells (which are CD127−).

### Lineage exclusion markers (CD14, CD33, CD64, CD34, CD19, CD15, CD66b, CD123)

Protocols use a cocktail of lineage antibodies to exclude myeloid cells, B cells, dendritic cells, basophils, and haematopoietic progenitors before gating on ILCs. Specific cocktail composition varies by laboratory.

> "Subsequently we gated out non‐relevant lineages using antibodies against CD3, CD4, TCRαβ, and TCRγδ (T cells), CD19 (B cells) CD14 (monocytes) FcRεR1a (mast cells), BDCA2 (pDCs), CD123 (pDCs, basophils), and CD34 (hematopoietic progenitors)."
— Krabbendam et al 2021 (PMID:33300130; DOI:10.1002/eji.202048696)

> "lineage cocktail (TCRα/β, TCRγ/δ, CD19, CD16, CD15, CD14, CD94, CD11c, BDCA-2, FcεRIα, glycophorin A, CD41, CD1a, and CD34)"
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

> "NK cells were excluded by adding anti-CD94 and anti-CD16 antibodies to the lineage cocktail."
— Alkon et al 2022 (PMID:34363841; DOI:10.1016/j.jaci.2021.07.025)

**Notes on individual markers:**
- **CD14** — present in all four lineage cocktails as a monocyte exclusion marker.
- **CD19** — present in all four lineage cocktails as a B-cell exclusion marker.
- **CD34** — used by Krabbendam et al and Alkon et al to exclude haematopoietic progenitors; consistent with SOULCAP's required exclusion.
- **CD15 / CD66b** — CD15 appears in Alkon et al's cocktail; no paper in this set listed CD66b explicitly (a granulocyte marker that can substitute for CD15).
- **CD123** — listed by Krabbendam et al as covering pDCs and basophils.
- **CD33** — not explicitly named as a lineage marker in any fetched paper; its role as a myeloid exclusion is complementary to CD14. CD64 is also absent from the lineage cocktails documented here, though both are standard additions in more extended panels.

## Markers with no direct evidence found

| Marker | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| CD33 | Not listed in any lineage cocktail in the four fetched papers. Its myeloid-exclusion role is well established in the broader flow cytometry literature but not quoted here. |
| CD64 (FcγRI) | Not listed in any lineage cocktail fetched. CD16 (FcγRIII) is used instead by Alkon et al to exclude NK cells/monocytes. |
| CD66b | Not found in any fetched paper. CD15 covers a similar granulocyte population. |
| ILCp-specific (CD117+CD56+CD294−) | No paper in this set discusses ILCp as a distinct gated population with this exact combination; the ILCp is referenced only in passing by Bonne-Année et al as a possible explanation for circulating CD117+ cells. |

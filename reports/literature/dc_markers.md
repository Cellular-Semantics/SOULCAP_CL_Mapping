# DC Marker Literature Support

Evidence for SOULCAP dendritic cell (DC) marker definitions, extracted verbatim from
open-access papers. Paywalled papers are noted in the Sources table but not quoted.

All block-quoted text was copied verbatim from the fetched page content; no paraphrasing
has been applied. Table-cell values from Table 1 of Rhodes et al. are reproduced
exactly as they appear in the published table.

---

## Markers with evidence

### CD11c / ITGAX (required high on cDC; low on pDC)

Biological context: CD11c (integrin alpha X, encoded by *ITGAX*) is the classical
surface marker of conventional DCs. cDCs express CD11c at high levels; pDCs are
CD11c-low or negative. SOULCAP requires CD11chi on all cDC gates and uses CD11clo
to help identify pDC.

> "Conventional DCs (cDCs), also known as myeloid DCs, can be defined as CD11c+ CD123−"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "pDCs are CD11c− CD123+ cells best characterized for their type I interferon"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "CD11c, a 'classical' cDC marker, can be downregulated on DCs"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

> "human pDCs (defined as CD45+CD11cloHLADRhi cells) were IRF8hiIRF4int across tissues"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

> "For DCs, we sampled LIN−HLA-DR+CD14− cells across the CD11C+ fraction (to enrich for
> CD141+ and CD1C+ cDCs) and the CD11C− fraction"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

---

### HLA-DR / MHC class II (required positive on all DC subsets)

Biological context: HLA-DR (MHC class II) is expressed at high levels on all mature
human DCs and is used as a pan-DC positive selection marker in combination with lineage
exclusion.

> "DCs were defined as live, LIN(CD3, CD19, CD56)−CD14−HLA-DR+ cells."

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

> "Numerous studies have shown that human dendritic cells express high levels of major
> histocompatibility complex (MHC) class II (e.g., HLA-DR)"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

> "human pDCs (defined as CD45+CD11cloHLADRhi cells)"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

Note: In Table 1 of Rhodes et al. 2019, cDC2-A entries show HLA-DR as "HLA-DR++",
while pDC entries show "HLA-DRlo", confirming differential expression across subsets.

---

### CD1c / BDCA-1 (cDC2 positive; cDC1 negative)

Biological context: CD1c (also called BDCA-1) is the canonical positive marker for
cDC2 (the most abundant blood cDC). cDC1s do not express CD1c.

> "DC2 and DC3 correspond to new subdivisions of the CD1C/BDCA-1+ cDC2"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

> "Although both cDC2 subsets can be characterized by CD1c expression, cDC2-A (CD32b+)
> are distinguished"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "CD172ahiCD1chi cDC2s"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

> "In the blood, DC subtypes include CD11C+ conventional DCs (cDCs), consisting of
> either CD141+ or CD1C+ cells"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

---

### CD141 / BDCA-3 / Thrombomodulin (cDC1 positive marker; lo/− on cDC2)

Biological context: CD141 (BDCA-3, thrombomodulin) defines the cross-presenting cDC1
subset in human blood. cDC2s express low or absent CD141.

> "DC1 corresponds to the cross-presenting CD141/BDCA-3+ cDC1, which is best marked
> by CLEC9A"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

> "cDC1s can be best identified by expression of C-type lectin-like receptor (Clec)9A
> and cell adhesion molecule 1 (CADM1)...and also express high levels of CD141 and XCR1"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

---

### CD172a / SIRPα (ideal positive on cDC2; negative on cDC1)

Biological context: CD172a (signal regulatory protein alpha, SIRPα) marks cDC2 and
is absent from cDC1. It is used in combination with CD1c and CADM1/XCR1 to cleanly
separate the two cDC lineages.

> "using CADM1 or XCR1 in combination with CD172a to separate cDC1s from cDC2s"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

> "CD172ahiCD1chi cDC2s"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

Rhodes et al. 2019 Table 1 (verbatim table-cell values by subset):

| DC subset | SIRPα (CD172a) |
|-----------|----------------|
| cDC1      | SIRPα−         |
| cDC2-A    | SIRPα+         |
| cDC2-B    | SIRPα+         |

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019, Table 1 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

### FceR1a / FCER1A (ideal positive on cDC2; also on basophils — see CD193)

Biological context: FcεRI (the high-affinity IgE receptor alpha chain, FCER1A) is
expressed on cDC2s, where it was found as a unique transcript marker of the CD1c+ DC
population. It is also expressed on basophils, which must be excluded separately (see
CD193/CCR3 below).

> "CD1C+ DCs (DC2 and DC3) expressed unique markers (e.g. CD1C, CLEC10A, FCER1A,
> FCGR2B, and CD1D"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

Note: This quote lists *FCER1A* as a gene-level marker of CD1c+ DCs (cDC2 lineage),
consistent with its use as a phenotypic marker in SOULCAP. Because FceR1a is also
present on basophils, SOULCAP employs CD193 (CCR3) or CD303 to exclude basophils
from the cDC2 gate.

---

### CD123 / IL-3Rα (high on pDC; low/negative on cDC)

Biological context: CD123 (IL-3 receptor alpha chain) is expressed at high levels on
pDCs, in contrast to the CD123-low/negative expression seen on cDCs. It is therefore
used both to positively select pDCs and to exclude basophils (which also express CD123
at high levels, hence the additional CD303 criterion in SOULCAP).

> "Conventional DCs (cDCs), also known as myeloid DCs, can be defined as CD11c+ CD123−"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "pDCs are CD11c− CD123+ cells best characterized for their type I interferon"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "Traditionally defined as CD123+ BDCA-2+ cells, pDCs have also been redefined by
> single-cell RNA sequencing"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "plasmacytoid DCs (pDCs), consisting of CD123+ cells"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

---

### CD303 / BDCA-2 / CLEC4C (pDC marker)

Biological context: CD303 (BDCA-2, CLEC4C) is a C-type lectin receptor expressed
selectively on pDCs. Together with CD123, it forms the canonical two-marker pDC
definition and distinguishes pDCs from basophils (which are CD123-high but CD303-low).

> "pDCs (e.g. IL3RA/CD123, CLEC4C/CD303)"

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

> "Traditionally defined as CD123+ BDCA-2+ cells, pDCs"

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

> "CD123hiCD303hi (human) pDCs"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

---

### CD33 / SIGLEC3 (high on cDC; negative on pDC — used to distinguish)

Biological context: CD33 (Siglec-3) is a myeloid surface antigen expressed at high
levels on cDCs and intermediate levels on some DC3/DC4 subsets, but absent from pDCs.
SOULCAP uses (CD33hi|CD64+) as an inclusive criterion to positively select cDCs while
allowing either marker to qualify.

Rhodes et al. 2019 Table 1 (verbatim table-cell values by subset):

| DC subset  | CD33     |
|------------|----------|
| cDC1       | CD33+    |
| cDC2-A     | CD33+    |
| cDC2-B     | CD33+    |
| CD16+ DC   | CD33int  |
| Axl+ DC    | CD33int  |
| pDC        | CD33−    |

— Rhodes JW et al. 2019, Table 1 (DOI:10.3389/fimmu.2019.01088)

Note: The table shows CD33 as positive on all cDC subsets and negative on pDC,
supporting its utility as a myeloid/cDC-enriching marker in the (CD33hi|CD64+) gate.

### CD64 / FcγRI / FCGR1A (optional positive for cDC; shared with macrophages)

Biological context: CD64 (FcγRI) was historically considered a macrophage-restricted
marker, but published evidence shows that cDCs also express CD64 at detectable levels,
making it unsuitable as a negative marker to exclude macrophages from the DC gate —
and conversely supporting its use in SOULCAP as part of an inclusive OR gate
(CD33hi|CD64+) for cDC identification.

> "In human and macaque, CD64 cannot be used, as cDCs also express some CD64."

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

> "CD64 is highly expressed on Macs"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

Note: The first quote confirms that cDC expression of CD64 is a documented fact
rather than contamination. SOULCAP exploits this by using CD64 positivity as an
alternative to high CD33 to capture cDCs; macrophage contamination is controlled by
the CD11chi HLA-DR+ requirement together with CD14−/CD16− exclusion.

---

### CD14 and CD16 (exclusion markers on cDC and pDC gates)

Biological context: CD14 and CD16 are classical monocyte surface markers. Their
exclusion from the DC gate is essential to prevent monocyte contamination. CD16 (FCGR3A)
marks a discrete DC4 population in some classification schemes but is excluded in
the SOULCAP cDC and pDC definitions.

> "DCs were defined as live, LIN(CD3, CD19, CD56)−CD14−HLA-DR+ cells."

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

> "CD14loCD16lo in human and macaque"

— Guilliams M et al. 2016 (PMID:27637149; DOI:10.1016/j.immuni.2016.08.057)

Note: The Guilliams quote describes the CD14loCD16lo criterion applied when gating
non-macrophage DCs in human blood and tissue, confirming the exclusion strategy.

Additionally, Villani et al. 2017 identify a separate DC4 population marked by CD16:

> "The DC4 cluster mapped to the CD141−CD1C− population and was accurately delineated
> by FCGR3A/CD16."

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

This population (DC4/CD16+ DC) is excluded from the SOULCAP cDC, cDC1, and cDC2
definitions by the CD16− requirement, consistent with its distinct transcriptional
profile.

---

### CD3 / CD19 / CD56 / CD127 (lineage exclusion panel)

Biological context: The standard "lineage dump" removes T cells (CD3), B cells (CD19),
NK cells (CD56), and ILC/lymphocyte progenitors (CD127) before gating DCs, preventing
cross-contamination from non-DC populations.

> "DCs were defined as live, LIN(CD3, CD19, CD56)−CD14−HLA-DR+ cells."

— Villani AC et al. 2017 (PMID:28428369; DOI:10.1126/science.aah4573)

Note: The SOULCAP pDC gate additionally requires CD127− (IL-7Rα) to exclude ILCs and
plasmacytoid-like lymphoid progenitors, which Villani et al. do not include in this
specific gating line but which is established practice for pure pDC isolation.

## Markers with no direct evidence found

### CD193 / CCR3

CCR3 is expressed on basophils and eosinophils, and its use as an exclusion marker
to remove these cells from DC gates — particularly the pDC gate, which relies on
CD123-high expression shared with basophils — is a standard flow cytometry practice.
However, none of the open-access papers fetched for this report contained a verbatim
sentence describing CD193/CCR3 in the context of DC gating or basophil exclusion.
Evidence will be added when a suitable open-access reference is located.
